Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I update my company profile?

Open Company Profile or Settings from the dashboard and update the available company details. If a field is not available yet, submit a support ticket and tell us what you need to change.

How do I create an invoice?

Open Invoices from the sidebar and use the invoice form to select or enter the customer, add the item or service details, set the dates and amount, then save the invoice. Check the invoice status after saving.

Why doesn't every lead include an email or phone number?

Lead sources only return information that is publicly available to them. Some records do not contain a verified email address or phone number. Treat missing details as unavailable—not proof that the person or business has no contact information.

What does a lead score mean?

A lead score is an estimate based on the available information and selected criteria. It is not a guarantee that a prospect will buy. Missing information should be treated as an information gap, not automatically as a bad lead.

Why might my dashboard show zero or incomplete figures?

Check that you are signed into the correct account and that the relevant records have been saved with the right dates and statuses. If the numbers still look wrong, open a ticket and include the page, date range, and the figures you expected. Do not include passwords or secret keys.

How do I track a support ticket?

After submitting a ticket, scroll to My Support Tickets on this page. Your reference number, priority, submission date, and current status will appear there.
